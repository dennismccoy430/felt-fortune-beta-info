Beta support

For beta support and privacy questions, email dennismccoy430@gmail.com, or use TestFlight feedback.

Report a problem

Include the version and build, iPhone model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Crop unrelated personal information from screenshots. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Known beta issue

Casino audio may intermittently stop after switching apps and returning. This issue remains open for monitoring, although it has not been reproduced recently by the owner. Please note the other app or activity, whether a call, video or Bluetooth audio was involved, and whether an action restored sound.

Protect your saved table

Game progress is stored locally. Before deleting or reinstalling the app, remember that local progress may be lost.

The game uses virtual chips only. No real-money wagering, prizes, purchases or advertisements.
