Privacy policy

Publisher and contact

Dennis McCoy operates this beta. Privacy and support questions: dennismccoy430@gmail.com.

The game name is provisional.

Information used by the game

The game runs locally on your device. It stores virtual-chip balances, wagers, hands, game history needed for Review Hand, and settings such as sound and play mode on the device. These saves are not sent to the developer by the game. The app does not create an online player account, provide an online leaderboard, or sync saves through a developer-operated service.

No advertising or tracking

The current beta contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, no tracking technologies used to follow you across apps or websites, no purchases, and no real-money gambling. Its bundled Capacitor components provide local app lifecycle and preferences storage. Game sounds are included in the app and do not require microphone access. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple operates TestFlight separately from this game. Apple may process installation, usage, diagnostic, crash and feedback information under its TestFlight terms and privacy practices and make beta reports available to the developer. See https://testflight.apple.com/ and https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ . This policy does not replace Apple's policies.

If you voluntarily send feedback through TestFlight, email or a text message, the developer receives what you send, which may include contact information, screenshots, device details, and reproduction steps. This is used to investigate beta problems and respond to you. Do not include passwords, unrelated personal details, or sensitive information in screenshots. The developer does not ask testers to provide email addresses for enrollment; the planned invitation method is a limited TestFlight link shared individually after approval.

Storage and deletion

Saved game data remains in the app's local storage. Removing the app may remove local progress; iOS backup behavior is controlled by your device and Apple settings. To request deletion of feedback held by the developer, contact the email above and describe the report. The developer will retain feedback only as needed to investigate issues and maintain relevant development records; Apple's handling and deletion of TestFlight records is governed by Apple.

Information-page hosting

When these information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy practices. This website hosting is separate from the offline game.

Audience

This is a simulated blackjack beta, intended for adults who meet Apple's applicable regional age rating. Virtual chips have no cash value and cannot be bought or redeemed. The beta is not intended for children.

Changes

This policy describes the current beta. If data practices change, this policy and the app's privacy disclosures will be updated before distributing those changes.
